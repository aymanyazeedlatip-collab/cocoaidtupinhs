# COCO-AID v2.2 — Daily Motion, Weather GIS, and Pest Photo Update

## Major changes

- Daily visual forecast frames through 2050, linked to weekly agricultural control points.
- Playback calibrated to seven daily frames per second, equivalent to one simulated week per second.
- Visible wind-particle animation over the climate-conditioned projection.
- Production chart defaults to a normalized trend index so Coconut w/ husk, Mature, and Young show their different weather responses; absolute-ton mode remains available.
- Rainfall, temperature, humidity, cloud, wind, and pressure charts open in a focused window around the selected date.
- Weather GIS moved to its own workspace and now interpolates all forecast layers between hourly model fields.
- Minimalist precipitation, temperature, cloud, pressure, and wind icons.
- Higher-visibility Weather GIS wind particles.
- Pest cards use locally bundled field photographs with credit, license, and offline illustration fallback.
- Pest recommendations use a two-sided 3D card flip and corrected overflow behavior.
- Formal PDF and Word reports now end at numbered Section 10; technical provenance and limitations are retained as an unnumbered subsection.

## Scientific note

Daily long-term frames are visualized plausible climate-conditioned conditions. They are not exact weather forecasts for future calendar dates. Agricultural production remains controlled by the annual and weekly stochastic farm model.
