# COCO-AID Tab Voice-Line Scripts — Phase 11.3.21

These lines are intentionally short and written to introduce each workspace immediately after the user opens it.

## Home
Welcome to COCO-AID. Start here to map your farm, review the workflow, and begin a complete coconut farm analysis.

## Farm Profile
This is your Farm Profile. Define the farm boundary, coconut trees, soil, management, and field observations used by every analysis.

## Long-Term Weather Forecast
This is the Long-Term Weather Forecast. Explore short-term provider weather and climate-conditioned farm projections through 2050.

## Extreme Weather Events
This is Extreme Weather Events. Review detected hazards, their forecast conditions, timing, severity, and possible effects on your farm.

## Intercropping Potential
This is Intercropping Potential. Compare crops under your coconut canopy, adjust light conditions, and explore the farm in interactive 3D.

## Farm Rehabilitation
This is Farm Rehabilitation. Inspect farm health, locate priority areas, and review the recommended rehabilitation sequence for your coconut trees.

## Pest Risk Analysis
This is Pest Risk Analysis. Compare pest and disease pressure, inspect the evidence, and identify which threats need attention first.

## Decision Support Network
This is the Decision Support Network. See how forecasts, pests, intercropping, and rehabilitation evidence combine into traceable farm recommendations.

## Farm Database
This is the Farm Database. Review the farms, forecasts, analyses, and reports that COCO-AID has saved for continued planning.

## Assisted Report Generation
This is Assisted Report Generation. Turn the latest analytical results into structured reports that can be reviewed, saved, and shared.

## About
This is About COCO-AID. Explore the research architecture, analytical methods, evidence boundaries, equations, and the system behind every recommendation.

## Weather GIS
This is Weather GIS. Explore the live atmospheric map and genuine short-term forecast data synchronized with your selected farm.
