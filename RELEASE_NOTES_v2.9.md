# COCO-AID v2.9.0 — Audio and Preview Experience

## Added

- Animated technological preview portal shown before the main workspace.
- `Click to Open Website` entrance that satisfies browser audio-gesture requirements.
- Bundled looping background soundtrack.
- Personal-agent narration for Home, Farm Setup, Farm Site Forecast, Extreme Weather, Farm Health, Reports, Database, About, Settings, Weather GIS, and forecast completion.

All v2.8 mathematical, GIS, forecasting, reporting, database, rehabilitation, and CoCO-PILOT functions are preserved.
