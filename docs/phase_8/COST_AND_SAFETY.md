# Cost, Labor, and Safety Policy

All Phase 8 costs and recovery effects are transparent development assumptions stored in `rehabilitation-scenario-parameters-1.0.0` and `rehabilitation-cost-catalog-1.0.0`. They require local price, labor, and field validation.

Safety rules:

- Predicted and suspected hazards are not confirmed damage.
- Inferred pest probability does not authorize chemical treatment.
- COCOAID does not generate pesticide or fertilizer dosage.
- Irreversible actions require field confirmation.
- Replanting requires mortality verification and planting-material validation.
- Drainage work requires safe outlet and erosion review.
