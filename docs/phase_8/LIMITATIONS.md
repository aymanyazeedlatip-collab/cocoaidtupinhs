# Phase 8 Limitations

- Cost, labor, crop-value, recovery, and risk-reduction coefficients are development assumptions.
- Scenario ranking is not field-validated causal optimization.
- Intercrop economics are gross-revenue scenarios, not net profit.
- Multiple pest losses may overlap; the engine uses risk summaries rather than naïvely adding every conditional loss.
- Cell inputs must be verified in the field.
- No pesticide or fertilizer dosage is generated.
