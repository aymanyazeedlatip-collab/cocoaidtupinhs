# Phase 10 Limitations

- CoCO-PILOT explains existing results and does not create agronomic evidence.
- Optional AI rewriting is not a scientific model and may be rejected by validation.
- Reports reproduce saved analytical data but do not guarantee field outcomes, income, or recovery.
- PCA source metadata supports traceability; expert validation of encoded parameters remains required.
- Long-term climate-conditioned paths are not exact weather forecasts.
