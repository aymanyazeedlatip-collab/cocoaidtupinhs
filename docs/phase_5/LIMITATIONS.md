# Phase 5 Limitations

- Transition probabilities and prior distributions are transparent development assumptions pending longitudinal field calibration.
- The simulator is experimental and is not an official PCA diagnosis, warning, or guaranteed production forecast.
- Farmer-reported evidence receives lower weight than field- or expert-confirmed evidence.
- Evidence likelihood functions are initial engineering formulations and require calibration against observed error distributions.
- Historical weather values obtained through forecast-provider archives are not equivalent to quality-controlled station observations.
- Particles are summarized for storage efficiency; individual particle histories are not persisted.
- Monthly transitions are a practical first implementation and do not represent every coconut physiological process.
- The Phase 4 baseline model was trained on synthetic/reference-derived data and remains a major source of uncertainty.
- Intervention effects are scenario assumptions until validated against implemented farm actions and measured outcomes.
