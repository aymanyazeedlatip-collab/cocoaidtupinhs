# Phase 9 Limitations

- Phase 9 consolidates existing analytical outputs; it does not create new weather, harvest, pest, or field evidence.
- Recommendation thresholds are transparent development rules pending expert validation.
- A high-priority recommendation is not a confirmed diagnosis.
- Cost, yield, pest, and intercropping outputs inherit the limitations of their source engines.
- CoCO-PILOT integration and formal narrative report generation remain Phase 10 work.
