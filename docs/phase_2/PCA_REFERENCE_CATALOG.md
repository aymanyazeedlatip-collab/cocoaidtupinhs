# PCA Reference Catalog

Catalog version: `phase2-pca-reference-1`

The Phase 2 seed contains:

- 15 registered source documents, one of which is restricted PII
- 30 coconut varieties: 12 tall, 12 dwarf, and 6 hybrid
- 408 variety parameter records
- 5 PCA pest or disease profiles
- 17 pest evidence rules
- 14 management-action records
- 35 intercrop candidates
- 81 canopy-light transmission records
- 2 fertilization scenarios

These records are reference parameters and decision rules. They are not represented as farm-level training observations.

## Verification status

Variety tables and brochure tables were transcribed from the supplied PCA materials and linked to their source document and page/panel. Records with ambiguous or broad ranges retain explicit confidence and review notes. Expert validation remains required before these parameters are used for official recommendations.
