# Limitations

1. PSA values are provincial aggregate production, not measurements from the selected farm.
2. Completed annual values through 2025 are official PSA records. Annual 2026 and unavailable cells are estimates based on available data and seasonal patterns.
3. Agricultural ML artifacts use a reference-based development dataset and require real-farm validation.
4. The compact long-term climate layer is a development projection layer, not a farm-scale observation or a substitute for processed CMIP6 products.
5. Exact cloud paths, rain fields, heatwaves, typhoons, or harvest values cannot be forecast through 2050. Long-term dates are plausible scenario-conditioned trajectories.
6. The selected hazard timeline represents one sampled future path. It should be interpreted with the posterior probabilities and intervals, not as a calendar of guaranteed events.
7. Weekly production values allocate annual simulated output for visualization. They are not guaranteed harvest amounts for the displayed week.
8. Pest likelihood ratios, transition probabilities, intervention effects, and utility costs need expert and field calibration.
9. Public soil or terrain estimates cannot replace laboratory testing and measured survey data.
10. Within-farm rehabilitation cells remain uniform when no measured spatial layer is supplied.
11. Live weather, map tiles, and browser libraries depend on internet/provider availability and provider terms.
12. COCO-AID is a research decision-support prototype, not an official prescription or warning service.

13. Phase 5 Bayesian priors, transition matrices, observation likelihoods, and intervention effects are transparent development assumptions requiring longitudinal calibration.
14. Predicted and suspected observations are not assimilated; farmer reports are assimilated at reduced reliability and still require field verification for high-stakes action.
15. Posterior particles are summarized instead of stored individually, and monthly transitions do not represent every coconut physiological process.
