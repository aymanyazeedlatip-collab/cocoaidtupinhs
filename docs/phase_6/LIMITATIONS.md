# Phase 6 Limitations

- PCA source documents provide qualitative factors, not calibrated likelihood ratios or local prevalence priors.
- Spatial-decay and severity coefficients are transparent development assumptions.
- Weather history from Phase 3 is archived provider data rather than a local station record.
- The production-loss baseline inherits the Phase 4 model's synthetic/reference-based training limitation.
- A probability does not confirm pest identity.
- Chemical dosage is intentionally excluded.
- Multi-pest expected losses overlap and are not independent.
- The Asiatic palm weevil profile is not merged with the legacy red palm weevil profile.
