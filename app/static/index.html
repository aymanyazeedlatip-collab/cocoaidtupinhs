Research principles

Built to show both the answer and where the answer came from.

01◎

Farm-centered

Every analysis is anchored to the farmer's mapped boundary, palm population, production baseline, observations, and selected farm context.

A shared farm identity prevents production, weather, pest, intercropping, and rehabilitation results from becoming disconnected analyses.
02◒

Uncertainty-aware

Bayesian updating and Monte Carlo simulation express uncertainty as probabilities, distributions, and ranges instead of guaranteed outcomes.

Long-term projections are explicitly climate-conditioned modeled futures, while genuine short-term forecast data remain separately labeled.
03▦

Evidence-linked

Analytical records preserve data source, model/parameter versions, scenario, simulation seed, and dependency identifiers for traceability.

A recommendation can be traced backward to the forecast, Bayesian evidence, pest assessment, intercropping run, and rehabilitation record that produced it.
04♧

Agroecosystem-aware

COCO-AID treats coconut production, canopy structure, intercrops, pests, weather, soil, and rehabilitation as interacting parts of one farm system.

Intercropping is screened against canopy light and competition rather than being presented as a generic list of companion crops.
05⌬

Decision-oriented

The architecture ends in ranked farm actions and formal reports, not isolated model scores.

The system integrates production, pest pressure, suitability, intercropping, and rehabilitation evidence before generating narrative guidance.
06!

Responsible use

COCO-AID supports planning and inspection. It does not replace agronomists, laboratory diagnosis, field validation, or local extension guidance.

Development assumptions and synthetic/reference-based validation remain disclosed wherever they materially affect interpretation.

Bayesian evidence updateP(H|E) = P(E|H)P(H) / [P(E|H)P(H) + P(E|¬H)P(¬H)]Updates pest-outbreak probability after symptoms and environmental evidence.
Bayesian odds combinationO(H|E₁…Eₙ) = O(H) × ∏ LRᵢCombines multiple evidence likelihood ratios under the stated conditional-independence assumption.
Beta posteriorp | D ~ Beta(α + successes, β + failures)Represents uncertainty in recurring rates such as outbreak incidence or replanting survival.
Farm-state transitionSₜ₊₁ = f(Sₜ, Xₜ, Uₜ, θ) + εₜMoves young, productive, aging, stressed, infested, recovering, and dead tree states through time.
Production responseYₜ = Nproductive × ybase × Fclimate × Fsoil × Fhealth × Fmanagement × FeventLinks productive tree population and multiplicative farm-condition factors to annual output.
Land suitabilityS = Σ wⱼ μⱼ(xⱼ)Combines normalized soil, terrain, rainfall, temperature, drainage, and stress memberships.
Wet-day processP(Zd = 1 | Zd−1, month, scenario, location)Preserves seasonal and consecutive wet/dry-day behavior in plausible future weather paths.
Wet-day rainfall amountRd | Zd = 1 ~ Gamma(k, θ)Generates non-negative rainfall amounts with realistic right-skewed variability.
Temperature persistenceTd = μd + φ(Td−1 − μd) + εdModels day-to-day temperature persistence around a climate-conditioned mean.
Monte Carlo recovery probabilityP̂(recovery) = (1/M) Σ I(YT ≥ threshold)Reports the share of simulated futures that meet the defined rehabilitation target.
Severe-loss probabilityP̂(loss) = (1/M) Σ I(YT < severe-loss threshold)Quantifies the chance that final production falls beneath the selected critical level.
